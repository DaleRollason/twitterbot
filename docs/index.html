Your details & calorie target

Your target uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation for resting energy, times your activity level, then adjusted for your goal. Change any field and it saves.

General guidance only, not medical advice. The target never drops below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) without a doctor's input.

Claude API key

Photo estimates call Claude with your own key from console.anthropic.com. It's billed to that account at roughly 1–2 cents a photo. The key is stored only on this phone and is sent only to Anthropic.

Backup

Your log lives on this phone. Back it up now and then, or before clearing your browser data or changing phones.
